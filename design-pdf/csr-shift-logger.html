Why entries are editable

The inquiry lifecycle

Entries aren't frozen. The key case: an inquiry that becomes an order later. That one editable field is what makes conversion measurable — with zero external data.

Conversion % = inquiries marked Order placed ÷ all inquiries — computed by the app, never typed.

Before we build

Decisions to approve

Six choices lock the design. My recommendation is marked on each.

1 · Storage backend Recommend: Supabase
A shared backend is required (many CSRs writing, one combined view). Supabase = hosted Postgres, instant APIs, no servers to run.
2 · Action catalog
The 6 confirmed are in. Which of the 6 candidates (new order, delivery, assigned-to-designer, extension, completed, spam) become buttons too?
3 · Sign-in Recommend: name + team passcode
Pick-your-name + one shared passcode to open the app (light). Or per-CSR login (stronger attribution, a bit more friction).
4 · Edit window Recommend: same business day
Entries editable through the current shift / business day, then locked — so history stays trustworthy.
5 · Check-in time Recommend: auto
Auto-stamped on "Start shift", check-out auto on submit. (Or let them type it.)
6 · CEO dashboard
Build the CSR input app first (Phase 1), add the CEO/lead read-dashboard as Phase 2 — or both together?
Next: approve this flow (or redline it), answer the six above, and share the full list of actions your CSRs actually do — then I build Phase 1: the input app, Supabase schema, and the auto-summary. Nothing is built until you say go.
