01 The big idea

Two apps that share one place

CSRs use one app to write down their work. The CEO uses a different app to watch. Both save to the same place online — so when a CSR adds something, the CEO sees it right away.
The CSR app
Pick your name, shift and profile, then write down each thing you do. You see your own counts.
🔑 One team password
save / read
The CEO app
Watch every CSR live. Look only — can't change anything. CSRs don't get this one.
🔒 A different password
DB
  One shared online database (Supabase)
A CSR taps save → it lands here → the CEO's screen updates on its own. No refresh needed.

In plain words

  • Nothing comes from old sheets or ClickUp. What the CSRs type is the only source.
  • Two apps, two passwords. CSRs can't open the CEO app.
  • Everything is live — new work shows up for the CEO straight away.

07 Step 4

Clocking out

One button ends your shift and writes your finish time. This is the only thing that sets it — so your hours stay correct even if you fixed things along the way.
You keep adding and fixing things
your start time stays the same
Press “Submit & clock out”
the only thing that sets your finish time
Finish time is written down · shift is locked
your hours = finish time − start time
Your report is saved and the CEO can see it

The simple rule

  • Submit = clock out. Nothing else writes your finish time.
  • Going back to fix a line does not change your finish time.
  • Once you submit, the shift is final and rolls into the CEO's totals.

What's saved

  • One shift row (who · when · note for next · the numbers).
  • One action row for each thing logged; its fields sit in details.
  • Counts are worked out from the action rows — never typed.

How it's built

  • React + Vite on Vercel — two small apps (CSR, CEO).
  • Supabase database with live updates.
  • CSRs can add/read; only the CEO app reads the live team totals.
